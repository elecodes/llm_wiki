# Programs

**Summary**: Age-grouped tennis lesson programs from beginner fundamentals to high-performance junior clinics.

**Sources**: [[tennis-kids-lessons-and-summer-camps-san-francisco]], [[parent-and-me-classes]]

**Last updated**: 2026-04-19

---

## Little Champs — Ages 4-5

- **Duration**: 1 hour tennis lessons
- **Level**: Beginner
- **What they learn**: Basic hand-eye skills, basic racquet skills, familiarization with tennis

## Champs — Ages 6-8

- **Duration**: 1 hour tennis lessons
- **Level**: Beginner to Intermediate
- **What they learn**: Basic techniques for fundamental tennis strokes, consistent hits over the net

## All Stars — Ages 9-12

- **Duration**: 1 hour tennis lessons
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Court**: 60' courts with orange balls
- **What they learn**: Extended rallies, intermediate footwork, net play, serves, how to play points, keep score, basic strategy

## Teens / High School — Age 13+

- **Duration**: 1 hour tennis lessons
- **Level**: High Performance
- **What they learn**: Rally with intention, spins, varying speeds and angles, play in transition, move to net
- **Grouping**: Grouped by age AND skill level. Priority to keep teens with age-appropriate peers, not younger kids.

## Additional Programs

- **What they learn**: Basic tennis skills together in a fun, age-appropriate setting
