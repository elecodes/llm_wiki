## Spring Season Tennis Lessons

Our Spring season runs **through May 31st** and is structured in a **lesson package format** to ensure steady development.

### Private Lessons
*   **Cost**: $120 per lesson, plus a one-time $25 setup fee.
*   **Benefits**: Ideal for fully personalized instruction and custom-paced learning.
*   **Current Availability**:
    *   Tuesdays: 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM
    *   Thursdays: 5:30 PM

### Group Lessons
If you are interested in group lessons, please provide [STUDENT NAME]'s age and skill level, and we will share the best-fitting available groups.

### Location & Logistics
All tennis lessons take place at the **San Francisco State University (SFSU) Campus**.
Upon registration, you will receive a welcome email with additional details, including directions and a **free parking permit** for campus parking.

### Benefits for Registered Students
*   **Make-up Lessons**: If you miss a lesson during the Spring season, you can schedule a make-up lesson later throughout the year.
*   **Priority Enrollment**: Registered students receive priority when signing up for each new season and for our Tennis Kids summer camps.

## SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp 2026

Don't miss out on the **SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp** at the San Francisco State University Campus! This program offers fun, weekly tennis camps for kids and teens aged **5–17**.

*   **Options**: Full-day, half-day, and day-pass options are available.
*   **Discount**: Take advantage of an **Early Bird $100 discount!**
*   **Benefits**: A great way for young players of all levels to improve skills, make new friends, and enjoy a lifetime sport in a supportive, energetic environment.

Register today while space is still available!
