# SF TENNIS KIDS Club: Spring Tennis Lessons

Thank you for your interest in the SF TENNIS KIDS Club!

## Spring Season Details

Our **Spring season** runs **through May 31st** and is offered in a **lesson package format**, designed to provide consistency and steady progress throughout the season.

### Private Lessons

*   **Cost**: $120 per lesson plus a one-time $25 setup fee.
*   **Ideal for**: Fully personalized instruction and custom-paced learning.
*   **Current Availability**:
    *   Tuesdays at 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM
    *   Thursdays at 5:30 PM

### Group Lessons

If you’re interested in group lessons, please let us know [NAME]'s age and skill level, and we’ll share the available groups that best fit.

### Location & Registration

All tennis lessons take place at **SFSU, San Francisco State University Campus**. Once registered, you’ll receive a welcome email with additional details, including directions and a **free parking permit** for campus parking.

### Benefits & Policies

*   If you can’t take a make-up lesson during the Spring season, you can schedule it later throughout the year.
*   Registered students have **priority when signing up for each season** and for our Tennis Kids summer camps.

## SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp 2026

Don’t forget to check out the **SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp 2026** at San Francisco State University Campus! It offers fun, weekly tennis camps for kids and teens:

*   **Ages**: 5–17
*   **Options**: Full-day, half-day, and day-pass options available.
*   **Discount**: Includes an **Early Bird $100 discount**!
*   **Benefits**: A great way for young players of all levels to improve their skills, make new friends, and enjoy a lifetime sport in a supportive, energetic environment.

Register today while space is still available!
