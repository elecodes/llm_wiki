# SF TENNIS KIDS Club: Tennis Lessons for 7-Year-Olds at SFSU (Spring Season & Other Options)

Thank you for your interest in SF TENNIS KIDS Club! This document outlines the available tennis lesson options for a 7-year-old at the San Francisco State University (SFSU) campus.

## Spring Season Details

*   **Season Dates:** March 2nd through May 31st
*   **Format:** Lessons are offered in a package format to ensure consistency and steady progress throughout the season.

## Lesson Options & Rates

### Standard Group Lessons

Based on a 7-year-old's age and skill level, the following group lesson options are available:

*   **Saturdays:** 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM
*   **Mondays:** 2:40 PM – 3:40 PM
*   **Mondays:** 5:15 PM – 6:15 PM

**Pricing:**

*   **Rate:** $56.25 per lesson (offered in a lesson package)
*   **Setup Fee:** One-time $25 setup fee, from your starting date.

### Other Lesson Package Options

*   **Semi-Private Lessons (2 students)**
    *   **Rate:** $82.50 per lesson per student
    *   **Setup Fee:** One-time $25 setup fee
    *   *Ideal for focused coaching; consider this if [CHILD'S NAME] has a friend who would like to join.* 

*   **Triad Lessons (3 students)**
    *   **Rate:** $65 per lesson per student
    *   **Setup Fee:** One-time $25 setup fee
    *   *Available upon request as a lesson package. Offers a balanced mix of individual attention and group interaction.*

*   **Create Your Own Group (minimum 4 students)**
    *   **Format:** Offered in a lesson package.
    *   **Discount:** The organizing family receives a **15% discount** off the standard group rate.

*   **Parent & Me Lessons**
    *   A wonderful way to learn together, these rates are based on the number of participating family members:
        *   **2 family members:** Semi-private lesson package rate — $82.50 per lesson per student + one-time $25 setup fee
        *   **3 family members:** Triad lesson package rate — $65 per lesson per student + one-time $25 setup fee
        *   **4 family members:** Group lesson package rate — $56.25 per lesson per student + one-time $25 setup fee

If you are interested in semi-private, triad, create-your-own group, or Parent & Me lessons, please specify your preferred days and times.

## Location & Logistics

*   **Location:** San Francisco State University Campus (SFSU)
*   **Parking:** Upon registration, you will receive a welcome email with additional details, including directions and a **free parking permit** for campus parking.

## Additional Benefits for Registered Students

*   **Make-up Lessons:** If you can’t take a make-up lesson during the Spring season, you can schedule it later throughout the year.
*   **Priority Enrollment:** Registered students receive priority when signing up for each new season and for our Tennis Kids summer camps.

## SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp 2026

Consider joining the **SF TENNIS KIDS Summer Camp** at San Francisco State University Campus, offering fun, weekly tennis camps for kids and teens ages 5–17.

*   **Options:** Full-day, half-day, and day-pass options available.
*   **Special Offer:** An **Early Bird $100 discount!**
*   **Benefits:** A great way for young players of all levels to improve skills, make new friends, and enjoy a lifetime sport in a supportive, energetic environment.
